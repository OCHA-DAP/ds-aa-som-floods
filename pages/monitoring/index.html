How to read this page

  • Four windows, one source each. Gu (March to May) runs on Google Flood Hub, Deyr (October to December) on GloFAS. A window activates when, on a single forecast day, enough of the river's points are forecast at or above their own return-period level: Juba needs 3 of 4 points, Shabelle 2 of 3. Any one window activating releases the allocation.
  • Action reads leads 1 to 7 days on the window's source. Readiness reads GloFAS at leads 8 to 12 days for every window, with levels fitted on that lead band, and releases only the mobilisation share.
  • SWALIM. A SWALIM moderate flood risk alert for either river also activates readiness. Bulletins are not read automatically: check FAO SWALIM alongside this page.
  • A window is open from twelve days before its season begins, when a forecast day can first fall inside it, until the season ends. Outside the two seasons the pipeline still runs and this page still updates, but no emails are sent.
  • Full design and evidence: the trigger summary and the analysis page. Which GloFAS version is live, and what it does to the thresholds: GloFAS version switch.
