How to read this page

  • Four windows, one source each. Gu (window February to June) runs on Google Flood Hub, Deyr (window September to January) on GloFAS. A window activates when, on a single forecast day, enough of the river's points are forecast at or above their own return-period threshold: Juba needs 3 of 4 points, Shabelle 2 of 3. Any one window activating releases the allocation.
  • Action reads leads 1 to 7 days on the window's source. Readiness reads GloFAS at leads 8 to 12 days for every window, with thresholds fitted on that lead band, and releases only the mobilisation share.
  • SWALIM. A SWALIM moderate flood risk alert for either river also activates readiness. Bulletins are not read automatically: check FAO SWALIM alongside this page.
  • A window is open by calendar month: Deyr from September to January, Gu from February to June. Every forecast day inside the window counts, so monitoring for Deyr starts in September and for Gu in February. In July and August the pipeline still runs and this page still updates, but no email is sent.
