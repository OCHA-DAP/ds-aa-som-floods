Somalia Riverine Flood Trigger

Trigger design for anticipatory action against riverine flooding on the Juba and Shabelle rivers. Three global streamflow models — GloFAS, Google Flood Hub and GEOGloWS — compete station by station against SWALIM river-gauge observations; the winners form a per-basin forecast consensus with a readiness leg at 7–12 days and an action leg at up to 6 days.

The proposed trigger — two candidate designs

Multi-source consensus

Each window votes on (station, model) pairs drawn from the five SWALIM gauges that still report, with all three providers competing freely and a station allowed to carry more than one model. Joint basin calibration: 1-in-3.2 overall, 1-in-4.3 / 5.2 per basin. Includes the SWALIM-threshold check, the impact tilt and a no-Google variant.

/trigger/T. Downing · Aug 2026

The balanced trigger

The same open multi-model consensus on the five still-reporting gauges, but with the activation probability balanced across basins and seasons as an explicit constraint rather than left to fall out of the search. Seasons come out exactly level; basins within one activation; 7 of 7 severe years and no activation outside the benchmark.

/balanced/design variant · Sep 2026

One model per window

Each river-season window runs on a single forecast source rather than a mixture, monitoring all seven reporting-era points and with no threshold below 1-in-3. Google carries Gu, GloFAS v5 carries Deyr; GEOGloWS is an alternative view. Same 1-in-3.2 envelope. Carries twelve open review notes behind a toggle.

Mixed models vs one model per window

Both setups on GloFAS and Google only, both on the five reporting gauges, both balanced so each basin-season carries the same activation probability at 1-in-3.2. Two outputs: the year-by-year trigger record, and accuracy (F1, TPR, PPV, lead time) against the gauges and against a target list built from CERF allocations and major EM-DAT years.
