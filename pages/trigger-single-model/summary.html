Somalia Riverine Flood Trigger / summary

The case for the trigger, in numbers

Four river-season windows, each on the one forecast model that tracks that river in that season best. A window activates when enough of the seven monitored gauges are forecast above their own return-period level on the same day. Scored against the SWALIM gauge record, 1999 to 2023. Full analysis: trigger analysis page.

What runs live, and what is still open
  • GloFAS v4 runs both phases today. Deyr is calibrated on GloFAS v5, which has no published forecast yet; Gu on Google Flood Hub, to which there is no API access yet. v4 stands in with levels refitted on its own record.
  • Bank full at a gauge is the fail-safe. It would have activated in 4 seasons no forecast caught, including Gu 2023 on the Shabelle; where a forecast also activated, bank full came 3 to 14 days later. Coverage, not lead time.
  • Two gauges have stopped reporting. Bardheere from 30 November 2023, Bualle from 14 March 2024.
  • Impact years are not yet defined. The benchmark is gauge levels, not people affected. 2013 (activation, no gauge flood) and 2021 (bank full at one gauge, 400,000 affected, no activation) are why an impact cross-check is the next step.
