New Google model: re-running the model choice

The comparison page keeps the adopted design and swaps the new Google model in where Google is used (the two Gu windows). Here the design's choice of model for each river-season window is re-run with the new Google model as a candidate, alongside GloFAS versions 4 and 5. The monitoring points stay the seven SWALIM gauges: unlike Nigeria, Somalia never selected its points by correlation. Everything on 1999–2023. Analysis of 4 November 2026.

1The short answer

  • Tracking. The new Google model follows the river gauges more closely than GloFAS in all four windows. The change is largest in Deyr, where the old Google model was weak (0.60 and 0.62) and the new one is not (0.87 and 0.87). That weakness is why Deyr went to GloFAS.
  • The design's search, re-run. With the design's two caps (activate in at most 8 of 25 years, release in at most 8 river-seasons), the search hands the new Google model only Gu Juba; GloFAS version 5 takes the other three windows. With the year cap alone, the new model also takes Gu Shabelle and Deyr Shabelle. Deyr Juba stays on GloFAS version 5 either way.
  • Forecast lead in Deyr. On the Shabelle, the new Google model's forecasts met the rule before GloFAS version 4's in both Deyr flood seasons of the shared archive (2019, 2020). On the Juba there is one Deyr flood season to judge by (2017): GloFAS met the rule well ahead and neither Google model met it.
Where the search and the design's own principle disagree. Under both caps the search moves Gu Shabelle from Google to GloFAS version 5, which tracks that window worst of the three (0.71 against 0.86 for the new Google model). It does so only because GloFAS activates in 2005, a flood season, which lifts the count from 7 to 8 years. The design's stated rule was that where the backtest cannot tell models apart, tracking and forecast lead decide. That points to the 7-year option: the adopted design with the new Google model in both Gu windows (1-in-3.7, all severe years, no activation outside a flood season). Which to adopt is for the working group.

The search's own measure, used to break ties: mean best-lag rank correlation of the window's points with the river's reference gauge (Luuq on the Juba, Belet Weyne on the Shabelle), 1999–2023. 3The design search, re-run

For every window the search tries each model, a station return period from 1-in-3 to 1-in-6 and a vote count from two to all-but-one points, with every window activating at least twice. It then takes the combination of the four windows with the most activation years under the caps, and among those the one that catches most severe years, has fewest activations outside a flood season, the fewest unanimous rules, the best tracking and the lowest return periods. Run on the old models it reproduces the adopted design exactly. Under both caps, the new-model pick is the only model assignment with its backtest score.

4Forecast lead

The other half of the design's judgement: which forecast reaches the rule first. For each window, the adopted rule (points and return period as designed) on each forecast archive against that model's own levels, at leads of 1 to 7 days, in the gauge flood seasons all three archives cover (Gu 2016–2023, Deyr 2016–2022): the first forecast issue that met the rule, and how long before the second gauge crossed its own 1-in-3 level. The GloFAS archive is issued about twice a week (104 issues a year), Google's daily, which can cost GloFAS up to four days here. The live pipeline reads Google at leads 1–5 (see the comparison page).

5Method

  • Candidates exactly as in scripts/model_selection.py (models, return periods 1-in-3 to 1-in-6, two to all-but-one votes, tracking against the reference gauge, every window at least twice), judged on the two-gauge benchmark of scripts/envelope_search.py. GEOGloWS is left out, as in the adopted design.
  • The two caps reconstruct the design's two decisions: activate no more often than 1-in-3 (8 of 25 years), and release in no more than 8 river-seasons (the change to Deyr Shabelle on 18 September 2026). With both, the search returns the adopted design on the old models, which the script asserts. The search is written out in scripts/google_model_selection.py; the pick of model_selection.choose is not used because it has no tracking tie-break and depends on candidate order.
  • Dollow is the Juba main-stem gauge, as live; the design ran on the Dawa-branch point and the reconstruction still returns the adopted design.
  • The Deyr windows were designed on GloFAS version 5 while the live forecast is version 4: see the GloFAS version switch.
